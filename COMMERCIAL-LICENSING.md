# Commercial licensing

This document explains Software.Land’s public commercial licensing policy for
`@software-land/search`. It is not a commercial license agreement and does not
itself grant commercial production rights.

## 0.5.0 and later

`@software-land/search` 0.5.0 and later, including 0.6.x, are source-available under the
[Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE) (SPDX: `BUSL-1.1`). License parameters in
[LICENSE](LICENSE) are unchanged.

- Non-production use is free under the standard BSL 1.1 grant.
- Production use is free under the Additional Use Grant when the consolidated
  annual gross revenue of you and your Affiliates for the most recently
  completed fiscal year is **less than USD $10,000,000**.
- Organizations at or above **USD $10,000,000** in consolidated annual gross
  revenue are not covered by that Additional Use Grant for production use and
  require a commercial license from Software.Land.

The free production condition is strictly less than USD $10,000,000. At
exactly USD $10,000,000, a commercial license is required.

Affiliate and consolidation semantics are defined in [LICENSE](LICENSE): the
threshold applies to you together with entities that control, are controlled
by, or are under common control with you.

## Historical versions (through 0.4.0)

`@software-land/search` versions through and including 0.4.0 remain licensed
under Apache License 2.0. Those grants are not revoked, altered, or
retroactively replaced by the 0.5.0 license change.

## Change License

Each BSL 1.1 version of `@software-land/search` eventually becomes available
under its Change License (Apache License, Version 2.0) according to BSL 1.1:
on the earlier of that version’s Change Date or the fourth anniversary of
first public distribution of that version under BSL. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Future versions may set different license parameters prospectively.

## Standard commercial pricing

When a commercial license is required, Software.Land’s standard public pricing
policy is **0.01%** of consolidated annual gross revenue per year.

That is the same as:

- `0.0001 × consolidated annual gross revenue` per year
- 1 basis point of consolidated annual gross revenue per year

There is currently no cap, no minimum beyond that formula, and no
covered-product-revenue allocation.

Examples:

| Consolidated annual gross revenue | Standard annual license price |
| --- | --- |
| USD $10,000,000 | USD $1,000 / year |
| USD $100,000,000 | USD $10,000 / year |
| USD $1,000,000,000 | USD $100,000 / year |

The revenue threshold determines whether a commercial license is required. The
0.01% formula is the standard price once a commercial license is required. It
is not part of the BSL Additional Use Grant, and using the software does not by
itself create an automatic royalty obligation. Commercial rights are supplied
under a separate agreement with Software.Land.
